# Decision: Pre-PR reviewer replaces self-review and the in-run intent judge; post-merge review becomes a sampled audit (founder 2026-10-29, TASK-620)

## Summary
The code review moves before the push: after the quality gates, the Opus reviewer runs in the executor's confined checkout (6 m) and, in enforce mode, one session-resumed fix round (only with >= 12 m of task budget left, <= 10 m, one 5 m re-gate, reset to the pre-fix SHA on failure). Self-review and the in-run Haiku intent judge retire (both check the PR against itself; only the reviewer checks it against callers, producers and conventions). Post-merge review stays as a sampled audit (1 in N, forced when the pre-PR review was not an enforce-mode approve). A clean fix round merges on green with no approval hold; a still-REQUEST-CHANGES PR carries pilot-review-rc and escalates to awaiting_approval (escalate-only, like scope_guard).

## Context
Three calibration runs (bench post_merge_reviewer_calibration_2026-10.md) showed the post-merge reviewer at 32-39 % recall and 5 of 11 human-approved PRs escalated; each post-merge REQUEST-CHANGES cost a 25-45 min fix-issue cycle (four in a row 10-28). Founder asked why review after CI at all.
