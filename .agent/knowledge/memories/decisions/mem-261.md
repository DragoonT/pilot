# Decision: Fair-use ceiling $300 — 30-day ledger evidence

## Summary
Pilot Cloud fair-use ceiling = $300/tenant/month (2026-10-21), up from $100. Ledger evidence (box DB executions, 30 days, 324 runs): mean $0.96/run, p50 $0.52, p90 $2.32, max $16.10; 100 runs ≈ $96. At $100 the dollar cap and the 100-ticket count collide (one retry breaks the promise); at $300 the cap is 3× mean and only stops abuse; worst-case margin $499−$300−~$50 box ≈ $149. Expect 1.5–2× on legacy customer repos; recheck after 5 tenants. Source: project_pilot_cloud_positioning.md §4.
